# Casey Fields Boulevard – contribution plans (working map)

Published automatically from a private working repository; do not edit here, changes are overwritten.

**A working aid, not an official record.** Projects, costs and land areas are as printed in each plan's tables; the mapped
shapes are approximate. Check against the gazetted plan before relying on anything here. Not for levy, works-in-kind or
land-acquisition decisions.

**Use of generative AI:** prepared with generative AI assistance (Claude Code, Anthropic) from public plan documents and
open data, and reviewed by the owner.
